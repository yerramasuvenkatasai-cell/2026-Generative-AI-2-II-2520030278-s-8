# Data Source Policy

Only approved, non-confidential institutional fee circulars, notices, and policy documents may be used. Do not upload student records, payment receipts, credentials, bank information, or personal data.

For each document added later, record:

- document title and issuing department;
- publication date or academic year;
- approval/permission status;
- source URL or internal reference; and
- whether a redacted sample rather than the original is stored here.
