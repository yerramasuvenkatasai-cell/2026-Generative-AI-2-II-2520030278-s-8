## Slide 2 — Problem statement

- Fee information is scattered across notices and PDFs.
- Students and parents need quick, reliable answers.
- Language barriers and unsupported chatbot responses can cause confusion.

## Slide 3 — Societal need

- Makes official information more accessible to students and parents.
- Supports users who prefer a local language.
- Reduces routine fee-related queries to administrative staff.

## Slide 4 — Proposed solution

Question → retrieve relevant approved fee-document sections → generate source-grounded answer → show source reference.

- English plus one selected local language
- Source citation with every answer
- “Information not found” when the documents lack evidence

## Slide 5 — Feasibility

- Input: approved, non-confidential fee circulars and notices
- Components: text extraction, multilingual embeddings, vector database, LLM, web chat UI
- Initial scope: common fee questions and two languages

## Slide 6 — Novelty and evaluation

- Institution-specific, multilingual, source-grounded fee assistance
- Test answer correctness, retrieval relevance, citations, language correctness, and response time
- GitHub: abstract, literature survey, research-paper references, and repository structure ready
